Garlic Parmesan Pasta

Simple, cozy pasta made with garlic, butter, and Parmesan. This is a sample recipe to test the Cookbook website and recipe navigation.

Prep: 5 min · Cook: 15 min · Serves: 2

Ingredients

  • 6 oz dry pasta
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • ½ cup reserved pasta water, as needed
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Optional: chopped parsley

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta in salted boiling water until al dente. Reserve ½ cup pasta water before draining.
  2. Melt butter in a skillet over medium-low heat. Add garlic and cook for about 1 minute, without browning.
  3. Add the pasta and a splash of pasta water. Toss to coat.
  4. Remove from heat, add Parmesan gradually, and toss until creamy. Add more pasta water as needed.
  5. Season with salt and pepper, garnish with parsley if desired, and serve immediately.
